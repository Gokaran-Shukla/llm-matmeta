We compute the magnetic moments and magnetocrystalline anisotropy of bulk bcc Fe and fcc Ni using
density functional theory with the PBE functional and spin-orbit coupling, as implemented in VASP.
The calculated spin moments of 2.2 Bohr magnetons for Fe and 0.6 Bohr magnetons for Ni agree with
magnetometry data. We further use Wannier-based tight-binding models to evaluate the orbital Hall
conductivity of both metals.
